Say the yellow row out loud rather than hoping nobody asks: the evaluator is independent, but this particular query set was assembled by QLever's own authors. The author's verdict over all four benchmarks is still that QLever is the fastest of the four engines. Two caveats of his worth knowing: all four deviate from the SPARQL standard somewhere, and Virtuoso silently returns at most 1,048,576 answers per query, which accounts for most of its divergences on WDBench.
