An independent benchmark

Patel-Schneider, Benchmarking SPARQL Engines on Wikidata Queries, Wikidata Workshop at ISWC 2025. Wikidata of October 2024 — about 20 billion triples — 298 WDQS queries, 10-minute timeout, 192 GB of RAM.

engine median mean timeouts
QLever 103 ms 4,583 ms 0
Virtuoso 461 ms 14,645 ms 2
Blazegraph 511 ms 12,560 ms 1
MillenniumDB 588 ms 103,271 ms 43

Loading the same dump: QLever about 4.5 hours, Blazegraph over 10 days — and the first attempt failed.

All four engines deviate from the SPARQL standard in places. QLever is the fastest of the four.

3

Use this to make the point that the choice is not ours: an independent author, four engines, a benchmark we had no hand in. The median matters more than the mean here — the mean is dominated by a handful of very long queries, and the author says so explicitly, preferring the arithmetic mean to show server load rather than user expectation.
