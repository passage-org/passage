Why change the backend?

  • Blazegraph is no longer maintained
  • QLever is maintained, and six times smaller
  • 283 MB against 1.7 GB, for the same 10,916,457 triples
  • But it has no augmented B+tree
  • no rangeCount, no keyAt(rank)
  • which are exactly the two questions a continuation asks

1

Blazegraph carries tuple counts inside its B+tree nodes, so "how many matches" and "which match sits at rank k" are both a tree descent. Its own uniform sampler is built straight on keyAt. QLever has neither — that is the whole question in one line.
