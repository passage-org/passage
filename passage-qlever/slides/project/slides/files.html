Two things to point at. The per-block metadata lives at the END of the permutation file, not in the sidecar: the reader seeks to the last offset and deserialises it at start-up. And the .meta sidecar is 992 bytes for PSO but 32 bytes for SPO — because SPO has 521,585 relations that each live inside a single block and are addressed by an offset within it, so they need no entry of their own.
