Where a block ends

index-rows-per-block = 31,250 is a soft target. Three rules bend it, and they explain the counts on the previous slide.

  • A large relation starts a new block
  • PSO: 349 blocks of 31,250 rows, plus up to 85 relation boundaries → 366
  • Small relations share a block, filled to 1.5 × the target = 46,875 rows
  • SPO: 10,916,457 ÷ 46,875 = 233 → 234, for 521,585 relations

  • A block grows past the target rather than split triples equal but for their graph
