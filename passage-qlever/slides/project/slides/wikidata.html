Wikidata reached the same conclusion

The Wikidata Platform Team proposes QLever as the replacement for Blazegraph in the Wikidata Query Service. Their reasons, quoted:

“an open source project that has gone unmaintained since being acquired by Amazon in 2018”
“unable to scale to meet the growth targets of Wikidata”
“the cause of much of the volatility of the Wikidata Query Service”

when what
Oct 2025 – Mar 2026 benchmarking and production-replay testing of the candidates
November 2025 triple store evaluation report published
April 2026 contractor engaged for documentation and query rewriting
17–23 Oct 2026 Query Service Days: the migration from Blazegraph to QLever

Our backend problem is the same problem the largest public SPARQL endpoint is solving — which is why porting Passage to QLever is worth doing now.
