Row 0 spelled out: <…#type> · <…wsdbm/Product0> · <…wsdbm/ProductCategory11> — and a fourth column holds the graph.

That shape is why it compresses, and why the six permutations do not compress alike:

The counter-intuitive row is PSO. Its col0 has only 86 distinct values and compresses to nothing, yet the file is larger than SPO's — because what matters is the other columns: sorted by predicate, the subjects inside one predicate are a long ascending run but the objects are near-random, while SPO gets two low-entropy columns. Every number here was read from the index: the rows through the JNI bridge, the sizes with ls.
