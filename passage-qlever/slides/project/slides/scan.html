A scan, step by step

# what happens cost
1 Resolve each bound component to an Id in the vocabulary a lookup
2 Pick the permutation whose key order puts them first free
3 Binary-search firstTriple_ / lastTriple_ for the block range in RAM, no I/O
4 Walk numRows_ to the block holding the offset in RAM — this is the patch
5 Read and decompress that block’s needed columns one block, every time
6 Trim the first and last block, which may be partial a scan of the block
7 Stream the blocks in between, ~20 in flight fast and parallel

Steps 1–4 are free. Step 5 is the whole cost of opening a scan, and it does not depend on how many rows you then read.

Step 4 is the forty-line patch: skipped blocks are counted, never opened, and the guard is that a block's row count can only be trusted when the block holds no duplicates differing by graph. Step 5 is why a per-thread cache of decompressed blocks is worth 38x on scan opening.
